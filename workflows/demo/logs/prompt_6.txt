### Why

`split_length_fst` takes `{l : List α}` **implicitly**, and the only thing that determines `l` is unification with the current termination goal. When you write

```lean
exact split_length_fst (by simp)
```

the argument `by simp` is elaborated as a separate synthetic goal whose statement is still `2 ≤ ?l.length` — `?l` is an unassigned metavariable at that moment. `simp` has nothing to rewrite in a goal made of metavariables, so it reports "made no progress" instead of proving anything.
